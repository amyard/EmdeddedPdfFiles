In June 2015, Smith and her three children initiated negligence claims against the State of Victoria, contending that the State owed them a duty of care to prevent breaches of family violence intervention orders [0]. The plaintiffs, including Smith and her children, formalized these allegations in August 2015 by commencing negligence proceedings against the State of Victoria, specifically claiming that the State failed in its duty to prevent breaches of intervention orders issued for their protection [1][2]. This central legal issue—the alleged breach of the State's duty of care in the context of family violence intervention orders—formed the core of their negligence claim [0][1][2][3]. The legal proceedings advanced to the Supreme Court of Victoria, where, in August 2018, the court refused the State's request for a summary dismissal of the negligence claim, enabling Smith and her children's case against the State and the implicated Victorian police officers to proceed [4].
